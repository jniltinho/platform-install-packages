## Scope boundaries — do not inflate or lower

Task1.1 does not explicitly require a complete transitive legal-compliance SBOM,
external upstream commit-to-byte equivalence for every bundled file, runtime
execution of every entrypoint, every analyzer finding adjudicated, final licensing
approval or release permission. Those are not legitimate automatic extra gates
for this inventory task. Exact pinned bytes plus scoped declared metadata can
identify the observed component without asserting unverified upstream equivalence.

Conversely, a version/license column full of guessed values, a count instead of
entrypoint enumeration, or an unclassified candidate list does not satisfy the
original clauses. Missing metadata must be honestly recorded and reviewed at its
correct scope. An inventory records what is bundled and what is demonstrable; it
must not call all components fully attributed when declarations are unresolved.
Whether a particular explicit unknown is acceptable inventory disposition must
be decided against the original requirement and evidence, not silently changed
into completed attribution or automatically escalated into a legal investigation.

syntax/static finding classification. It can remain open after1.1 closes on a
complete reviewed inventory. Runtime entrypoint execution belongs to5.10 and
